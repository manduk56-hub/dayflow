편집 프롬프트: Edit this existing transparent pixel art asset. Remove ONLY the three purple floating bubble circles above the sleeping cat, replacing their pixels with full transparency. Preserve the cat, blanket, cushion, all outlines and colors, subject position, scale, original square canvas dimensions, and existing transparent background exactly. Do not redraw or restyle the cat or any other object. Do not crop, resize or shift the subject. The empty transparent space above the cat will be used for separate animated bubbles in an app. Output transparent PNG.

## 최종 생성 프롬프트

Create one small UI illustration asset for a Korean pixel retro productivity timer app called Dayflow. Transparent background, square canvas. A cozy sleeping cream-colored cat curled up on a mint green cushion, eyes closed, peaceful relaxed smile, a small lavender blanket over its back, tiny lavender sleeping bubbles above (no letters or text). Calm restful feeling immediately recognizable at 100px display size. Strict crisp chunky 16-bit pixel art, deliberately low pixel resolution aesthetic with clean stair-step edges, no antialiasing, no gradients, no soft shadows. Limited palette: cream #fffdf4, warm cream #e6d6b8, mint #d9ead4 and teal #367f77, lavender #b4a2da and purple #7560b2, dark plum outlines #39324b. Bold dark pixel outlines, simple compact silhouette, centered composition occupying 85% of canvas, minimal details. No clock, no interface, no border frame, no floor or scenery, no text. Final image is a standalone transparent sprite to be placed beside the rest countdown, not an app screenshot.
